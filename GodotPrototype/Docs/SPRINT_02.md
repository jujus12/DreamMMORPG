# Godot Prototype - Sprint 02

## Tablet controls
- Tap a training dummy to select it.
- Tap the ground to walk to that point.
- Use the bottom buttons for basic attack and the four Warlord active skills.
- Keyboard movement (WASD / arrow keys) and Space attack remain available when a keyboard is connected.

## Warlord starter kit
- Cleave: 35 damage to damageable targets within 3.5 m; 2 s cooldown.
- Overhead Strike: 60 damage to selected target within 3.2 m; 3 s cooldown.
- Whirlwind: 25 damage to targets within 4.5 m; 6 s cooldown.
- Charge: move toward selected target and deal 45 damage; 5 s cooldown.
- Passive: +5% ATK and +5% DEF from equipped items. This is documented as a character rule; equipment/stat calculation is not implemented yet.

## Known prototype limitations
- Placeholder capsules are used instead of final character art.
- The village is a greybox, not a finished environment.
- The current target feedback is textual; health bars, hit animations, effects and sound are next.
- This is not an online multiplayer build and has not yet been tested in the Godot Android editor.
